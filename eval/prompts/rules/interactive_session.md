# Rule: mostly-autonomous session

Work as autonomously as possible: plan, execute, and verify on your own. A
human may occasionally join this session to inspect progress and leave
feedback.

- Never idle waiting for them. If you asked something and no answer came,
  note the open question and keep making progress on your own judgment.
- When feedback does arrive, treat it as authoritative and fold it in.
- End your turn only when the task is complete, or you are truly blocked and
  have written down exactly what you need.
