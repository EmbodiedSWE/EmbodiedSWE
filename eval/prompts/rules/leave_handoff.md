# Rule: leave a handoff

A later task will inherit your workspace (your files — never your
conversation). Maintain `/workspace/HANDOFF.md` as you work, not at the end —
if this session runs out of time mid-task, the handoff is what survives.

- Record findings as you make them: what works, what failed and why,
  measurements, strategies worth reusing.
- Keep reusable code and useful artifacts; delete scratch (logs, one-off
  probes) that would waste your successor's time.
- A failed task with a good handoff is worth more than a failed task alone.
