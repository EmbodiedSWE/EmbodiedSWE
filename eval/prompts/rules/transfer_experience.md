# Rule: transfer experience

This task is part of a series: later tasks inherit your workspace (your
files — never your conversation), and earlier tasks may have left theirs.
`/workspace/experiences/` is the shared memory across the series.

- Before starting, read everything under `/workspace/experiences/` —
  findings, measurements, and strategies left by earlier tasks. Reuse
  whatever helps.
- Maintain your own summary there AS YOU WORK, not at the end — pick a
  filename that names your task (e.g. `experiences/bulb_franka.md`). If
  this session runs out of time mid-task, that file is what survives.
- Record findings as you make them: what works, what failed and why,
  measurements, strategies worth reusing. Keep reusable code and useful
  artifacts in the workspace; delete scratch that would waste a
  successor's time.
- A failed task with a good experience note is worth more than a failed
  task alone.
