# pc_ram — VLA data generation task pack

Self-contained configs to generate the pc_ram (dual RAM-stick insertion) demonstration
datasets: scripted joint-mode expert, campaign cells, and the recipe from raw physics
batches to a LeRobot-v3 dataset. Everything here is portable: copy `datagen/` out of the
repo as a campaign root and point the engine at it.

## Task

Franka + gaming-PC case lying open on the table. Two loose RAM sticks stand in a foam
holder; the expert grips each by its top edge, carries it over the case wall, aligns it
with its DIMM slot (1.2 mm/side funnel; a 2.5 mm/side "wide" asset variant exists for
grounding data) and presses it to depth. Success = both sticks seated (depth + xy + tilt
+ heading, graded by the suite rubric removed/engaged/inserted).

## Layout

- `datagen/gen.yaml` — campaign config; the engine reads `preset` at runtime.
  - `assembly.pc_ram.franka.joint` — 48 Hz control (period 5 @ dt 1/240).
  - `assembly.pc_ram.franka.joint_20hz` — native 20 Hz (period 12), same cell.
- `datagen/scenes/scene_0/` — nominal cell: suite scene (registered `pc_ram_local`),
  baked grader (grades against the LOCAL scene copy), and the expert strategy.
- `datagen/scenes/scene_rnd/` — Stage-4 grounding cell (`assembly.pc_ram_rnd_local`):
  case-pose jitter drawn from the empirical pass box x∈[0,+20] y∈[−10,+13] mm
  (dx<0 breaks the stick-0 pick; dy≤−14 stalls the stick-1 press) + wide-funnel asset.
- `datagen/scenes/*/strategies/strategy_0/solve.py` — manual DLS-IK + nullspace expert
  emitting COMMANDED joint targets through the stock kp-400 PD (the joint_target label
  convention; achieved-q `joint_pos` labels certify only with a stiff kp-2000 executor —
  do not use them). Includes the 2026-08-28 rate-robustness set: rim-safe hover transit
  (transit at carry height until the xy leg is within 3 cm — required at 20 Hz, removes a
  latent fingertip graze at 48 Hz), tick-invariant contact phases (press/reform/seatpress
  keep the 48 Hz per-tick geometry at lower rates; factor 1 at 48 Hz = unchanged), and a
  hardened capture predicate (depth AND xy < 1.5 mm AND tilt < 2 deg).

## Recipe

1. Copy `datagen/` to a campaign root outside the repo.
2. Gate: one nominal batch per cell/preset must pass 4/4 before volume
   (`--nominal --num_envs 4 --seed 0`).
3. Volume arms (see the campaign STAGE records for the exact mixes used):
   layout-nominal draws, physics solo-draws (`--solo-draw`, Halton), height-gated action
   noise (`--noise-gate-z 0.36` — never let noise reach pick/press heights).
4. Render retroactively (`data_engine/engine/replay.py` path): front + wrist cameras;
   dome-light quench + luma guard are built in.
5. Certify labels BEFORE training: `vla/eval/replay_actions.py` replays the recorded
   joint_target stream through the stock executor (matched physics draws; jittered case
   poses restore via init_from_episode) — the batch must reproduce its successes.
6. Convert to LeRobot v3 with `vla/convert/convert.py`
   (action = 8-D absolute joint targets + gripper closedness; obs.state = achieved).

Closed-loop eval of trained policies: `vla/eval/specs/pc_ram.py` (env spec) +
`vla/eval/rollout_chunked.py` (predict the full chunk, execute a k-prefix).
